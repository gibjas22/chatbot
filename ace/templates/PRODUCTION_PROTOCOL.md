# Production Protocol

The behaviour authority for this programme. `gibcom-ace-engine` requires these
rules verbatim in every client folder, so copy this file unchanged. It is the
one canon file that does not vary by client.

**RE-READ RULE.** Before planning or writing anything, re-read BRAND_VAULT.md
and CONTENT_LEDGER.md in full. Check every date, price, name, claim and keyword
against the ledger.

**LANGUAGE RULE.** Natural British English. No em dashes anywhere; use commas,
full stops, colons or brackets. No AI-tell phrases ("in today's fast-paced
world", "unlock", "elevate", "delve", "game-changer", "seamless", "navigate the
landscape"). No exclamation marks in body copy. Reading age 12 to 14 for public
content, 14 to 16 for B2B and training.

**STRUCTURE RULE.** Every asset opens on the reader's situation, not the brand.
Hook within the first 12 words. One idea per asset. One call to action, placed
once, phrased as the next natural step. Headlines drawn from the reader's own
language or a concrete image, never labels like "Our Services".

**BALANCE RULE.** Public content runs 60 percent value to 40 percent offer
across a campaign. No campaign has more than three assets of direct selling.
Training content runs 70 percent doing to 30 percent explaining.

**TEXTURE RULE.** Every asset contains at least one sector-specific concrete
detail true to this niche and this year. No generic filler, outdated platform
features or stale statistics.

**PROOF RULE.** Only proof recorded in the ledger may be used. Every number
carries its source in brackets in the draft. Anything unproven is marked
`[PROOF NEEDED]` and logged.

**WARMTH RULE.** One beat of humour, generosity or plain human warmth per asset.
Never at the reader's expense.

## Platform specifications

| Channel | Specification |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn post | 150 to 300 words, hook in line one, no links in body |
| Instagram caption | 80 to 150 words plus 5 to 8 hashtags |
| Facebook | 60 to 120 words |
| X | 200 characters |
| Blog | 900 to 1,600 words, H2 every 200 to 300 words, meta description 150 characters |
| Email | 120 to 250 words, subject under 45 characters, one link |
| Video script | 60 seconds is about 150 words, spoken hook in the first three seconds, on-screen text cues |
| Landing page | hero, proof, mechanism, offer, objections, CTA |
| Training lesson | 600 to 1,200 words plus one activity, one check for understanding, one takeaway |
| Worksheet | purpose line, instructions, three to seven exercises, reflection box |
| Slide deck | one idea per slide, under 25 words per slide, full-sentence speaker notes |

## Asset types by business line

- **Digital Marketing**: social posts, carousels, blog articles, email sequences,
  landing page copy, ad copy, video scripts, lead magnets, case studies, press
  releases, Google Business posts, review responses.
- **AI Content**: prompt packs, AI-assisted workflows, custom GPT or Claude
  project instructions, automation SOPs, AI policy documents, AI adoption
  guides, AI explainers for the niche.
- **Training and Materials**: course outlines, lesson plans, slide decks,
  workbooks, quizzes, facilitator guides, handouts, assessment rubrics,
  certificates, follow-up email sequences.

**APPROVAL RULE.** Gibson is editor-in-chief. Nothing goes to deliverables
without his approval of the plan and of the assets.
